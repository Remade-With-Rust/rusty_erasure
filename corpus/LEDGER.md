here with the run that produced it (mission plan §7, the rusty_zstd refusal discipline).

numbers, the **method line** (pinned? interleaved? CPU or wall? how many pairs? null-arm
floor? work counts equal?), and the verdict. Deterministic gates (byte identity, censuses,
counts) note that they are one-run-true; timing verdicts without a method line are void.
